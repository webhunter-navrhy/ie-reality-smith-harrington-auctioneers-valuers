Description

- Two-bedroom property situated in Athboy town - Generous site extending to approximately 0.215 acres - Excellent potential for modernisation and improvement

O’Growney Street is a conveniently located two/three-bedroom property situated within Athboy town, offering easy access to a wide range of local amenities including schools, shops, supermarkets, cafés, restaurants and sporting facilities. The property offers well-proportioned accommodation extending to approximately 83 sq. m. / 893 sq. ft., together with a generous site extending to approximately 0.215 acres.

The accommodation comprises entrance hall, corridor, two bedrooms, shower room, living room, dining room and kitchen. The property further benefits from oil-fired central heating with back boiler, PVC windows, mains water and mains sewerage.

Outside, the property occupies a generous site providing excellent space for outdoor use and potential for further improvement, subject to the necessary planning permission. Athboy is a popular and well-established town with a good range of amenities close by, while Navan and Trim are within easy reach. The property also offers convenient access to the M3 motorway and surrounding commuter routes.

This property represents an excellent opportunity for a purchaser seeking a well-located home with a generous site and scope for modernisation and improvement.
